TapMarker Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Overview

TapMarker is a user-controlled automation app. It lets users place markers, save presets, preview tap positions, and run automation using Android Accessibility Service.

Accessibility Service

TapMarker uses Accessibility Service only to perform actions configured by the user, such as taps, text input, and scrolling. The service is not used to collect, read, store, or share personal or sensitive screen data.

Data Collection

TapMarker stores automation presets locally on the user's device. These presets may include marker positions, delays, repeat counts, action types, and user-entered text for automation steps. TapMarker does not upload these presets to our servers.

Advertising

TapMarker may show ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers and related data according to Google's advertising and privacy policies.

Sharing and Export

If users export or share a preset file, that action is controlled by the user. Recipients should review imported presets before running them.
